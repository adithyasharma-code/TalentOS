# User Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the target users of TalentOS.

Understanding who we are building for helps guide product decisions, feature prioritization, user experience, and AI behavior.

---

## Primary Persona

### Persona Name

Mid-Career Professional

### Profile

- Experience: 5–8 years
- Employment Status: Currently employed or recently transitioned
- Industry: Primarily IT and technology (expandable to other industries)
- Goal: Secure a better role with improved compensation, career growth, or work-life balance.

### Characteristics

- Has an established resume and professional experience.
- Applies across multiple job portals.
- Has limited time to search and apply for jobs.
- Wants high-quality opportunities instead of applying everywhere.
- Values automation that saves time without losing control.

### Current Workflow

- Searches jobs on multiple platforms.
- Reads every job description manually.
- Tailors resumes manually.
- Repeatedly fills application forms.
- Tracks applications in spreadsheets, notes, or memory.

### Frustrations

- Repetitive application process.
- Multiple job portals with duplicate listings.
- Time spent tailoring resumes.
- Difficulty tracking applications.
- Uncertainty about which jobs best match their profile.

### Success Looks Like

The user can discover relevant opportunities, prepare optimized applications, and manage the complete job search from one platform while spending significantly less time on repetitive tasks.
---

## Secondary Personas

The following user personas are intentionally excluded from the MVP and will be considered in future releases.

### Fresh Graduates
Users entering the job market for the first time who require resume building and interview guidance.

### Career Switchers
Professionals transitioning into a new industry or technology stack.

### Returning Professionals
Professionals returning after a career break, relocation, higher education, or layoffs.

### Senior Leaders
Professionals with 10+ years of experience seeking leadership, management, or executive roles.

TalentOS v1.0 will not optimize for these personas. The product will first validate its value for the primary persona before expanding support.

---

## Persona Goals

The primary persona wants to:

- Find high-quality job opportunities without searching multiple platforms.
- Reduce the time spent tailoring resumes for each application.
- Avoid repetitive form filling across job portals.
- Apply only to roles that closely match their skills and career aspirations.
- Track every application from a single dashboard.
- Increase interview opportunities through better-targeted applications.
- Focus more on interview preparation than administrative tasks.

---

## Pain Points

The primary persona experiences the following challenges:

- Job opportunities are scattered across multiple platforms.
- Duplicate job postings waste time.
- Resume tailoring is repetitive and time-consuming.
- Application forms repeatedly ask for the same information.
- There is no centralized view of submitted applications.
- It is difficult to know which jobs are worth applying for.
- Manual job searching takes significant time every week.

---

## Success Criteria

TalentOS successfully serves the primary persona when they can confidently say:

> "I finally stopped juggling five different job portals."

This means the user can:

- Discover relevant opportunities from one place.
- Eliminate repetitive searches across multiple platforms.
- Manage every application through a single workspace.
- Spend more time preparing for interviews than managing applications.
- Feel confident that they are not missing relevant opportunities.

---

## Product Implications

Based on the primary persona, TalentOS should:

- Automatically discover jobs instead of requiring manual searches.
- Prioritize quality over quantity in job recommendations.
- Build a unified career profile from the user's resume and preferences.
- Group similar jobs into career clusters to reduce repetitive resume tailoring.
- Provide a single dashboard to manage the complete job search lifecycle.
- Keep the user in control of important decisions while automating repetitive work.
- Continuously improve recommendations based on user feedback and outcomes.
