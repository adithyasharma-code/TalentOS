# ADR-001: TalentOS is a Career Operating System

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-02

---

## Context

The initial idea for TalentOS was to automate job applications across multiple job portals.

During product discovery, it became clear that the larger problem is not submitting applications—it is managing the entire job search process.

Experienced professionals spend significant time:

* Searching multiple job portals
* Evaluating job descriptions
* Tailoring resumes
* Filling repetitive application forms
* Tracking applications
* Following up with recruiters
* Preparing for interviews

Existing platforms solve only parts of this workflow.

---

## Decision

TalentOS will be built as an **AI Career Operating System**, not as another job portal.

TalentOS will integrate with existing platforms and provide a unified workspace that helps users:

* Discover opportunities
* Match jobs intelligently
* Tailor resumes
* Automate repetitive tasks
* Track applications
* Prepare for interviews
* Analyze career progress

---

## Rationale

Building a Career Operating System provides a broader and more sustainable product vision than focusing solely on application automation.

This decision ensures that future capabilities naturally fit within the product, including:

* AI Career Coach
* Resume Intelligence
* Career Analytics
* Recruiter Relationship Management
* Learning Recommendations
* Interview Preparation

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Clear long-term vision
* Extensible architecture
* Strong product positioning
* Supports future AI agents

### Negative

* Larger product scope
* Requires phased delivery
* MVP must remain tightly focused

---

## Guiding Principle

>Your career deserves an operating system, not another job board.
